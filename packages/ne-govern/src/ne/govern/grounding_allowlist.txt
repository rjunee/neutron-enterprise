# Format: one token per line, lowercase, no punctuation. Blank lines and '#'
# comment lines are ignored. Sorted.

# Contents: articles, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, auxiliaries,

# verified, directory, table, ...). total/sum/count/number/value are included
# so a sentence like "the amount total is 4200" can ground (the numeral still
# needs a same-sentence provenance anchor from its own column/aggregate label
# — F3 hardening, §2.7 — these words are not themselves anchors).
#

#     top, better, worse, greater, greatest
#   modals: must, may, might, should, would, will, shall, could
#   quantifiers/approximators: all, every, only, some, many, few, several,
#     nearly, almost, roughly, approximately
#   spatial comparatives: over, under, above, below
# These exclusions mean the gate cannot assert free-text comparisons or
# quantified/approximate claims — it can only present grounded facts and let
# the reader compare (the fail-closed resolution of the relational-claim
# attack, design §2.3/§7.3).
