The transcript, before and after

Both panels are the live renders — scroll them, expand the step bands, and use each panel's own theme toggle. The changes, point by point:

  1. Turn boundaries. Before, one exchange ran into the next with nothing between them. Now a turn opens with the question bubble and closes with its receipt rule.
  2. Tool-call weight. Before, read.knowledge.search · Tier — · Allowed sat at full prominence directly above the answer. Now routine allowed steps collapse into one quiet work band with a count, names, verdict and time.
  3. The answer. Before it floated as unattributed full-bleed prose. Now it is the turn's only unboxed prose, at 15px, under the mark and the word Answer — nothing else in a turn gets that treatment.
  4. Governance in one place. Before, tier/allowed, completion/verification/model/trace, and feedback sat in three scattered grey rows. Now one receipt per turn carries outcome · provenance · model · trace · feedback.
  5. Collapse. Before, nothing folded, so a long run was unreadable. Now the work band is a disclosure: collapsed by default, the full step ledger one click away.
  6. Promotion. A step awaiting approval or refused no longer sits in the same grey texture. It leaves the fold as a gate card with the turn's only hue, holds the action, and never collapses.
