Ranking (design lead)

  1. A — Meridian. I would ship this. Round 1 failed by changing nothing, not by keeping the character — A moves every token that caused the flatness (six-step ladder, Plex/JetBrains, 4/8px geometry, real elevation, 40px rows) while keeping the two things that are actually brand: the cold hue family and the cherenkov accent. Best queue-clearing screen of the three. Caveat, stated honestly: it clears the stranger test with the smallest margin — "same brand, new product."
  2. B — Hairline. The most disciplined execution and the best Ask screen; instantly distinct from today. Second because it spends the product’s identity to get there — it reads as excellent generic modern software — and glyph-only status gives an approver a smaller scanning signal than tinted pills.
  3. C — Atelier. The strongest first impression; the Tier-3 wire-transfer sheet looks like a document worth signing. Third because it optimises the demo over the Tuesday afternoon: 44px rows cost density in every governance list, and warm-plus-gold is the largest brand departure.
