# Reg E Operations Bulletin (2026)

This bulletin applies to consumer debit-card electronic fund transfer claims. Open the case on the date Meridian receives notice, preserve the customer communication, document each investigation step, and record the final decision in the servicing case. Use Debit Card Dispute Handling SOP v4. Eligible disputes at or above $500 follow the 5-business-day expedited deadline. Oral notice starts investigation; signed attestation follows within 10 calendar days.
