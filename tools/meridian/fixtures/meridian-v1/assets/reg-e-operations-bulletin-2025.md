# Reg E Operations Bulletin (2025)

This bulletin applies to consumer debit-card electronic fund transfer claims. Open the case on the date Meridian receives notice, preserve the customer communication, document each investigation step, and record the final decision in the servicing case. Use Debit Card Dispute Handling SOP v3. All eligible disputes follow the 10-business-day provisional-credit deadline. A signed written statement is required before investigation.
